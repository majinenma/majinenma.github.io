Low Card Showdown: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Low Card Showdown is a card game published by MajinEnma ("we").

Accounts

You can play as a guest with no sign-up. If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your Google account's name, email address and an account ID from Google, and we store your account ID, name and credit balance with Google Firebase so your credits follow you between devices. To delete your account data, contact us at the address below.

Credits

Credits are free play-money used only inside the game. They cannot be bought, sold, transferred or exchanged for money, goods or prizes.

What is stored on your device

The name you type, your chosen character, your sound setting, your shop items, a guest's credit balance and a random player ID are saved in your browser or app storage on your own device. Clearing the app's data removes them.

Playing in a room

When you create or join a room, your typed name, character, random player ID and the cards in the round are sent through a message relay service so the other players' devices can show the game. Anyone who has the room code can join that room. Do not type a name you want to keep private. Room data is not used for advertising.

Relay provider: EMQX public MQTT broker (privacy policy).

Advertising

The game may show ads from Google between rounds. Google and its partners may use cookies or a device advertising ID to show and measure ads. See how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services. Buying "Remove ads" stops ad breaks in the game.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play. We receive a purchase token to confirm the purchase and unlock the item. We never see your card or bank details.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 18.

Contact
